# FrankenLedger — Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Real app only. No mockups, no generated video. Recorded by the entrant as a
screen recording of the real app. Sample data is fictional ("Noor General
Store") and labelled on screen.

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook
The monster-lab upload screen. Say: "My shop's books live in WhatsApp and
Excel. Both are lying to me." Show the four stitched technologies as four
body parts joining.

## 0:15–0:45 — The mess
Upload the fictional sample WhatsApp chat export (20 lines, Roman Urdu,
amounts, "kal paise dunga"). Show raw lines. Then upload the sample Excel
ledger: messy columns. Say: "Orders in chat, money promised in chat, ledger
updated by hand — late, partially, or not at all."

## 0:45–1:20 — The stitch
Click "Reconcile". Orders extracted with source-line links; the four
technologies shown joining. Say: "FrankenLedger reads both files, and every
fact links back to the exact chat line and ledger row it came from."

## 1:20–2:00 — The flags
Three flags appear on screen:
- `PAID_NOT_RECORDED` — Ahmed paid Rs 500, no ledger row.
- `RECORDED_NOT_PAID` — ledger says Sana paid, chat says udhaar.
- `PRICE_MISMATCH` — same order, chat Rs 500 vs ledger Rs 550.
Fix one flag on screen (accept); totals update live. Say: "Nothing changes
until I say so. Every flag has its evidence."

## 2:00–2:25 — The reply + the paper
Draft reply in Roman Urdu for an unpaid order, copied in one click. Then
download the daily ledger PDF and show it. Say: "A reply I can paste back
into WhatsApp — and a paper ledger for the shop counter."

## 2:25–2:45 — Close
Clean spreadsheet export. Honest close on screen: "Built with Kiro. Sample
data — fictional. No WhatsApp API, no payments." Repo URL + live app URL on
screen.

## Rules
- Real app only, under 3:00, public YouTube link.
- No prize / win / user claims anywhere in the video.
